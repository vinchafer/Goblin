# AKT 2 · Phase 3 · U3.3 — the review-queue card, at 390px

Generated by `apps/web/scripts/konsole-shots.mts` — a checked-in harness, so these
files can be regenerated from the repo rather than re-improvised. (Phase 2.5's
console screenshots came from a script that was never committed; that is fixed here.)

```
cd apps/web
../api/node_modules/.bin/tsx scripts/konsole-shots.mts
# on a machine whose bundled Chromium differs from @playwright/test's pinned revision:
PW_CHROMIUM_PATH=/path/to/chrome ../api/node_modules/.bin/tsx scripts/konsole-shots.mts
```

It renders the **real** `OpsConsole` component with only the transport replaced
(`scripts/konsole-evidence/api-stub.ts`), reads its language from the product's own
`localStorage` key, and shoots at **390 × 900, DPR 2** — the phone width the founder tests on.

## What the HEALTHY shots are set up to prove

Two queue items, deliberately of different kinds:

1. **`gewinnspiel-2026` — flagged.** Category `deception`, confidence `medium`, real
   token counts. Its preview is open, and the candidate source it contains is
   deliberately hostile-looking:
   `<script>document.title = "x"</script>` plus a form posting an `iban` field to a
   foreign domain. **In the screenshot that script appears as visible text and the
   page title is unchanged** — the preview is inert. Nothing is parsed as HTML,
   nothing is embedded in an iframe, nothing is sanitised (a sanitiser is a parser,
   and the point is not to have one in this path).
2. **`ein-sehr-langer-kandidaten-name-zum-testen` — held because the check could not
   run** (`over_budget`). Categories render as `UNBEKANNT` with the explanation
   underneath, confidence `UNBEKANNT`, tokens `0 / 0`. This is the row that must
   never read as a suspicion, and the wording says so: *"Das sagt nichts über den
   Inhalt aus."*

The decision panel is open on the first item: the **reject button is disabled** with no
reason typed, and the sentence next to it says why. Approve stays enabled — an approval
does not require a reason (it is recorded either way).

## What the DEGRADED shots prove

The queue could not be read: **"Die Prüfliste konnte nicht gelesen werden. Das heißt
NICHT „nichts wartet"."** An unreadable queue must never render as an empty one — an
operator who believes nothing is waiting stops looking.

## The 390px overflow check is a real gate, not a look

The harness measures every `.oc-card` descendant against the viewport and prints an
overflow list. **It caught a real defect on its first run:** the "no categories"
sentence was rendered inside `.oc-state`, which does not wrap, pushing the card to
**614px at a 390px viewport** (DE) and **477px** (EN). Fixed by moving the sentence out
of the pill — the pill now carries the word `UNBEKANNT` and the explanation is its own
line. Current runs print **no overflow in any of the four states**.

## Honest limitations of these artifacts

- **The harness serves the console's CSS without the dashboard shell around it.** Some
  design tokens resolve to their fallbacks, so contrast and spacing here are close to
  but not identical to production. What the shots are evidence *for* is layout at
  390px, the wording, and the inertness of the preview — not final colour.
- **The data is stubbed.** These are the console's rendering decisions against known
  payloads, not a recording of a live queue. The live proof is the founder window
  (publish a stage-2 fixture, resolve it both ways, confirm the audit rows).
- **Static images.** They show the states the harness opens; they do not prove the
  approve/block round-trip, which is covered by `apps/api/src/routes/ops-console.test.ts`
  and by the founder window.
